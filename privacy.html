Promptflow Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

What the app uses

Promptflow uses your iPhone's camera and microphone to record videos, Apple Speech Recognition to follow your script, and your Photos library to save recordings. All of this happens on your device.

Google Drive

If you connect Google Drive, Promptflow asks only for the drive.file permission. With it, the app can create a "Textream" folder in your Drive and upload the videos you record. It cannot see or change any other files in your Drive. The app reads your Google account email only to show which account is connected.

Sign-in tokens are stored in your iPhone's Keychain. You can disconnect at any time in the app's Settings, which deletes the tokens and revokes access. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

What we collect

Nothing. Promptflow has no server, no analytics and no advertising. Your scripts, recordings and Google data are never sent to the developer or to any third party. Videos go only to your Photos library and, if you turn it on, your own Google Drive.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
